Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

This application ("the App") is a personal content management tool used to plan, generate, and schedule fitness and wellness video content for a single TikTok account owned and operated by the developer.

Information We Access

The App accesses TikTok account data solely as necessary to publish video content on behalf of the account owner, using TikTok's official Content Posting API. This may include authentication tokens required to post content and basic account information needed to complete a post.

How Information Is Used

Data accessed through the TikTok API is used only to publish content to the developer's own TikTok account. It is not shared with, sold to, or accessed by any third party.

Data Storage

Authentication tokens are stored locally and securely by the developer for the sole purpose of maintaining the connection to their own TikTok account. No user data from other TikTok accounts is collected, stored, or processed.

Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party APIs (such as AI content generation services) to assist in creating video scripts. These services do not receive TikTok account credentials or personal account data.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated periodically. Continued use of the App constitutes acceptance of any changes.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact the developer through the associated TikTok Developer account.
